Where this data comes from — read me

This is a guide, not a definitive record. It's assembled from public sources, it's incomplete, and it's a live picture of a fast-moving story — figures, owners and plans change. Always check the linked official source before relying on any number.

The register (known projects) is compiled and maintained by The Scouse Oracle from public reporting, company announcements, local authority planning portals, national grid connection data and Find a Tender. Ownership flags are an editorial judgement about where a company is headquartered or controlled.

Live planning activity is pulled automatically each day from PlanIt, which aggregates planning applications from 400+ UK councils. These are raw, unverified applications — a filing is not a built data centre. Each links back to the official council record.

Locations and distances are approximate; postcodes are geocoded via postcodes.io. Investment figures are announced headline amounts, not local costs. Nothing here says any single project has raised your own bill.
